# Glassify

A floating glass music visualizer that sits on top of your desktop and moves with Spotify.

- **Windows:** download `Glassify.exe` from the [Releases](../../releases) page and double-click it. Needs Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11 and the Spotify desktop app. Right-click the glass, or its icon near the clock, to quit.
- **Mac:** build with `Glassify/build.sh` (macOS 14.2 or later).

`preview.html` is the original design. Both apps show the same page (`Glassify/Web/index.html`); `Glassify/` is the Mac app around it and `GlassifyWindows/` the Windows one.
