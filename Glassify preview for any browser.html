Glass Wake

Design preview of the floating widget. The music here is simulated; in the real widget the bars follow whatever Spotify is playing on your computer, and the cover comes from the current track.

Press a bar to push it down and send a wave out. Drag the small bar at the top to move the glass and the corner to resize it. The dots switch mode and the + opens that mode's settings.
